### The Chapter 9 revision (highest-impact change)

Chapter 9, "The Seventh Gate," materially revises the prior mythology:

- The party themselves perform the Rite with all seven Seals.
- The prison beneath the sanctuary opens.
- The **Black Vizier — a distinct being, NOT Qadir —** steps free.
- Qadir kneels to the Covenant with **"I have kept my promise,"**
  revealed as the Covenant's true keeper, serving an older promise.
- The demons answer to the Black Vizier.
- The Seals remain behind, their purpose complete, reclaimed by no one.
- The Djinn sacrifices himself to cover the party's escape; his fate is
  left unresolved.
- Ending emotion: **loss + resolve + hope**, not despair. (Supersedes
  the older "player believes they lost" ending in the Game Bible's Book
  One Goal.)

This is the ruling that puts the Mythology Bible (Spec 007) and Qadir's
character profile (Spec 009) under revision: **Qadir ≠ the Black
Vizier.**
