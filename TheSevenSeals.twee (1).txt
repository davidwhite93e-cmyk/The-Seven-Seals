CHAPTER 1 — SCENE 1
The Festival of Lanterns, dawn
==================================================*/

<<set $game.time = "Dawn">>
<<set $game.location = "Amir's Home">>

The call to prayer finds you before the sun does.

It drifts over the rooftops of Zafirah in long, unhurried waves, and for a moment you lie still and let it pass through you the way you have every morning since you were a boy. Somewhere below, your mother is already awake -- you can smell cardamom and scorched sugar, which means she is burning the pastries again, which means she is nervous, which means today is the Festival of Lanterns and half the street will be at your door by midday wanting bread they didn't have to bake themselves.

Your father's pendant is cool against your collarbone. It always is, first thing, before your own warmth reaches it.

Dust hangs gold in the one shaft of light that gets past your shutter. A rooster somewhere is losing an argument with a dog. Zafirah is waking up loud and unbothered, the way it always does, as if it has never once considered that a day could go wrong.

You lie there a moment longer, listening to the city.

Old Bilal has been making lanterns in this quarter since before your father was born, and every year he insists this is the last one, and every year he is lying.
