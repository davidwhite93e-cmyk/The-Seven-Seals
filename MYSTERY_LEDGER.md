## Active Mysteries (added Ch3)

- **The Unseen Searcher** — Player believes: an unknown rival is
  racing them to the old places. Truth: deliberately open — the
  generations-old search, the cut journal pages, and the scholars'
  anonymous patron may be one hand or several (candidates include the
  protective rival faction and Qadir's centuries-long erasure
  program); the Creative Director resolves this no earlier than Ch8.
  First hint: Ch3. Reveal: Ch8+. Status: Active.
- **The Cut Pages** — Who removed the damaged journal's final pages,
  and when. First hint: Ch3. Reveal: TBD. Status: Active.
- **The Escaped Scholar** — Player believes: a fleeing looter got away
  with copies. Truth: his incomplete copies travel down the pilgrim
  road and will eventually reach Qadir. First hint: Ch3 ("Not to
  them!"). Reveal: Ch9. Status: Active (truth concealed).

## Concealed Events (never hinted to the player)

Backstage-only entries — these must never surface in codex, journal, or
dialogue text ahead of their scripted reveal.

- **Shrine Restoration Funds Redirected** (Ch2) — Qadir quietly
  convinced the Sultan to redirect ancient-shrine restoration funds
  toward post-Ghūl village protection. Publicly this reads as
  compassionate crisis response; in truth it left the First Shrine
  isolated and forgotten, exactly as Qadir intended. No one is harmed
  directly, and no one suspects him. Player never sees this on-screen.
  Reveal: Ch9, retroactively, as part of Qadir's full confession.
  Status: Active (concealed).
- **The Right Records, the Wrong People** (Ch3) — Qadir ensured
  records pointing to the Sunken Library became available to ambitious
  scholars who would strip it; he neither ordered nor prevented their
  expedition. The escapee's incomplete copies will reach him via the
  pilgrim-road drop. Everything appears coincidental. Reveal: Ch9.
  Status: Active (concealed).
- **Maps for Collectors** (Ch4) — Qadir quietly ensured old maps
  describing the Fortress reached collectors who would pay for
  forgotten relics; greed hired the raiders. They were never expected
  to succeed — the point was to weaken the Fortress and force the
  Second Guardian to entrust the Seal sooner than he otherwise would
  have. Qadir commanded no one. Reveal: Ch9. Status: Active
  (concealed).
- **The Forgotten Maintenance** (Ch5) — Years earlier, funding meant
  for ancient infrastructure (the Covenant-era qanat beneath Three
  Springs among it) was redirected toward more immediate political
  concerns — a decision that seemed wise, necessary, compassionate.
  Qadir never touched the orchard; he understood which forgotten
  places would eventually fail and let time do the work. His hands
  stay clean. Reveal: Ch9. Status: Active (concealed).
