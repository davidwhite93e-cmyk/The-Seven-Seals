# Chapter 2

Purpose (per Design Spec 002): teach what a Seal is, teach how
Guardians work, establish that worthiness matters more than strength,
player earns the First Seal, Qadir quietly removes another obstacle.

## Implemented (Scenes 1-3 — the "Travel" phase of the Seal rhythm)

- Scene 1 — Hakim's Study: the shrine is named, Yasmina joins the party.
- Scene 2 — Departure: farewell to Farah, leaving Zafirah.
- Scene 3 — The Caravan: road to the hill country, optional NPC beats
  (Sabiha, the pilgrim, Yasmina).

## Awaiting Scene Package (Mystery -> Guardian -> Trial -> Reflection -> Seal -> Aftermath)

Blocked on `Ch2_Caravan_End`, which currently links to the
not-yet-written `Ch2_Desert`. Implementation cannot proceed past this
point without the Creative Director specifying:

- The First Guardian's identity and nature.
- The shape of the Trial (what "worthiness over strength" means
  mechanically — a combat encounter, a moral choice, a puzzle, or some
  mix).
- How the First Seal is physically/narratively earned and what it is.
- The specific, on-screen way Qadir "quietly removes another obstacle"
  in this chapter, per his Golden Rule (no direct on-screen causation).
